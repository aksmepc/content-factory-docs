# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11

This application ("Content Factory") is a private, personal-use tool that
automates content creation and publishing for my own YouTube channels.

## Data the app accesses

- **Google account authentication**: The app authenticates with my own
  Google account using OAuth 2.0 to obtain permission to manage my own
  YouTube channels.
- **YouTube data**: Through the YouTube Data API (scopes
  `youtube.upload` and `youtube.force-ssl`), the app uploads videos,
  sets thumbnails, and manages metadata for my own channels only.
- **Generated content**: Scripts, audio, thumbnails, and metadata are
  generated locally and stored on my local machine.

## What the app does NOT do

- It does not access any other person's Google or YouTube account.
- It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with
  third parties.
- It does not use YouTube data for advertising or analytics purposes.

## Data storage and retention

- OAuth tokens and generated content are stored locally on my own
  computer and remain under my control at all times.
- I may delete this data at any time by removing the local files.
